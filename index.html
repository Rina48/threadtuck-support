ThreadTuck support

ThreadTuck keeps a chosen number of recent conversation turns visible and lets you restore older turns when you need them.

Open a support issue

Before you report a problem

  1. Update your browser and ThreadTuck, then reload the affected tab.
  2. Pause and resume ThreadTuck from its popup.
  3. Include the browser version, ThreadTuck version, steps to reproduce, expected result, and actual result.
Protect your privacy. Do not post private conversation text, screenshots containing personal information, account details, passwords, or authentication tokens.

Quick fixes

The popup says “Open ChatGPT”

Open a supported page on chatgpt.com, then open the ThreadTuck popup again.

Older turns are not folded

Confirm ThreadTuck is enabled, choose a visible-turn limit, and select Apply to tab.

You want every turn visible

Select Show all hidden messages. ThreadTuck changes local visibility; it does not delete messages.

Privacy summary

ThreadTuck runs locally in your browser. It does not collect or transmit conversation text and it has no analytics, advertising, or extension-operated server.
